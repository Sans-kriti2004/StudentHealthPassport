## Slide 2: The Problem Statement
- **Slide Title**: The School Emergency Gap
- **Subtitle**: Why India's School Infirmaries Face Dangerous Treatment Delays
- **Key Realities**:
  - **82% of Schools** still rely on handwritten paper registers kept in locked administrative offices.
  - **1 in 8 Indian Children** lives with asthma, severe food allergies, or chronic conditions.
  - **64% of Emergency Calls** to working parents go unanswered on the first attempt.
- **The Core Danger**:
  - Information asymmetry leads to dangerous delays or lethal mistakes—such as administering penicillin to an allergic child or confusing diabetic hypoglycemia with heat exhaustion.
- **Speaker Delivery Script**:
  > *"The prompt poses a critical challenge: Indian school nurses are compassionate and skilled, but they are crippled by a lack of basic, immediate health information. When a child stops breathing or goes into anaphylactic shock, every second counts. Finding a key to a filing cabinet or searching through 2,000 paper files during lunch break is not just slow—it can be fatal."*

## Slide 3: The Innovation & Solution
- **Slide Title**: The Digital 1-Card Health Passport
- **Subtitle**: Consolidating Life-Critical Medical Data into One Scannable Touchpoint
- **Design Principles**:
  - **One Card, Zero Confusion**: Everything a nurse or teacher needs fits on a single, high-contrast digital card.
  - **Emergency Red Banner**: Prominent Blood Group badge (e.g. B+, AB+, O+) and critical allergy alerts at the very top.
  - **Step-by-Step Clinical Directives**: Numbered 1-2-3 instructions (e.g., Spacer technique for asthma, Glucose dosage for hypoglycemia).
  - **Medication Locker Locator**: Pinpoints the exact infirmary shelf or backpack location for inhalers, insulin, or EpiPens.
  - **1-Tap Dispatch**: Instant click-to-call for parents, attending pediatricians, and 108 emergency ambulance services.
- **Speaker Delivery Script**:
  > *"We asked ourselves: What does a first responder need in the first 60 seconds? They don't need a 20-page medical history. They need: What is the condition? What is the blood group? What medicines must NEVER be given? And what are the 3 steps to stabilize them right now? That is the Student Health Passport: one card, bold typography, instant clarity."*

## Slide 4: Technical Architecture & Indian School Realities
- **Slide Title**: Engineered for Real-World School Infrastructure
- **Subtitle**: Offline-First, Privacy-Compliant, and Instantly Deployable
- **Architecture Highlights**:
  - **100% Client-Side QR Engine**: Generates emergency vector SVG QR codes directly in the browser—no external internet API required.
  - **Offline Resilience**: Works without internet or cell service; critical for basement clinics, sports fields, or power cuts.
  - **Physical Lanyard Badges**: One-click generation of CR80-sized printable student ID badges with lanyard punch slots.
  - **Parental Consent Verification**: Cryptographically verified consent tokens ensuring HIPAA/DPDP privacy compliance.
- **Speaker Delivery Script**:
  > *"Many schools in India have spotty Wi-Fi, especially on the playground or in basement medical rooms. If an emergency system requires logging into a heavy cloud portal with a username and password, it will fail. Our Student Health Passport runs offline. Any smartphone camera can scan the lanyard badge and immediately render the emergency directives without downloading any app."*
